Before you write in

Questions we get most

How long does delivery take?
Within India: 3–6 working days once dispatched, and we dispatch in two. Free over ₹2,500, otherwise ₹120 flat. Worldwide: 7–14 working days by DHL from ₹1,900, duties payable by you on arrival.
Why does my piece look different from the photo?
Because a person made it. Wood grain, glaze pooling, dye depth and block registration all vary between pieces. We photograph a representative item, not the exact one you receive. If the difference is beyond that — a crack, a warp, a colour that is plainly wrong — send a photo and we will replace it.
Something arrived broken.
Email a photo of the piece and the box within 72 hours. Replacement or refund, your choice, and we do not ask for the broken item back. Around 1.4% of our ceramics break in transit; we pack without plastic and accept that cost.
Can I return something I simply don't like?
Yes, within 15 days, unused and in its original packing. Return shipping is on you unless the fault is ours. Made-to-order and personalised pieces are not returnable, and we flag those clearly on the product page.
Do you do wholesale?
From 12 pieces per SKU, with a 6–10 week lead time because the batch has to be made. Trade pricing on request at trade@xomexo.com. We do not do private label — the maker's name stays on the tag.
I'm an artisan. How do I sell through Xomexo?
Write to makers@xomexo.com with photographs of your work and the name of your cluster. We visit before we list anything, so it takes months rather than weeks, and we take on roughly four new families a year.
