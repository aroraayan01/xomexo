Why we started

The maker gets 62 paise of every rupee

In most of the handicraft trade, the person at the wheel sees somewhere between eight and fifteen percent of what you pay. That number is the whole reason Xomexo exists.

We buy outright and in advance, at a price the artisan sets. We publish what we paid on every product tag. It makes our margins thinner and our catalogue smaller — and it means the craft is still here in twenty years.
