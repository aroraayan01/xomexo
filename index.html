Why Xomexo

Bought directly from the artisan

Most handicrafts pass through three or four hands before they reach a shop, and very little of the price gets back to the person at the wheel. We buy straight from the workshop, pay half in advance and keep the artisan's name on the product.

That means a smaller catalogue and slower restocks. It also means the piece you receive was made by someone we have actually met.
